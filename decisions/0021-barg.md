# 0021 — Barg: an append-only ledger, earned by learning; a shop of looks at fixed prices

- **Date:** 2026-11-03
- **Status:** accepted
- **Deciders:** Claude, on PLAN 9.2–9.4 and the owner's session prompts; the owner can say otherwise

## Context

The game's one currency and its shop, before anything can be sold (9.1 needs a company). The owner: earned by
learning and playing well, never by time spent; fixed prices, cosmetic only, no random rewards
([players.md](../players.md)).

## Decision

- **A ledger, enforced by the database.** `barg_ledger` rows can't be edited or deleted (a trigger); a balance
  is the sum of the rows. Only one change exists: a merged guest's rows move to their account. A unique `key`
  makes each earning and purchase happen once, and after a merge the same reason and ref can't earn again.
- **What earns:** +5 a street guessed within 500 m for the first time; +50 every street or spot of a district
  learned; +20 first on a challenge's board against someone else, at most 3 wins in 24 hours, so two guests on
  one phone aren't a Barg machine. Nothing for playing, logging in or returning. **The daily challenge will
  earn through the same ledger once it exists (8.4).**
- **Streets already learned count:** the migration writes them into the ledger. The learning came first.
- **A street earns in practice too:** on "Adashgan ko‘chalarim" and in rooms; finding it is the learning.
- **The shop's list lives in the server** (`internal/barg`): ids and prices, so a page can't change a price. The
  looks and names live in the game. What a player wears is in `users.prefs`, set only by wearing what they own.
- **Prices:** pins and trails 60–80, frames 100–300. About 12–60 learned streets each: earned by learning a
  part of the city, not by grinding.
- **Not in the shop for now** (the owner, 2026-11-03): compass skins ("extra") and room emotes (real animation
  takes time). They were built and played; a copy waits in `tmp/parked-compass-skins-emotes/` on the laptop. The
  three basic compasses stay free. Whoever had bought one was paid back with a `refund` row: the ledger is
  never edited.
- **In a room a pin keeps its player's colour,** skin or not: that colour is how friends tell pins apart. Their
  trail goes with them: each line is its player's, on every phone. A player's colour moved from a ring round
  the face to a tiny pin on its corner, because a free gold ring looked just like the bought frame.

## Why

- A trigger, not a promise: when real money comes (9.5), a ledger nobody can edit is what makes refunds and
  audits honest.
- Learning pays, because that's what the game is for. A daily login bonus would pay for time, and players.md
  says the game doesn't try to eat people's time.

## Consequences

- The balance is summed on every `/v1/me`. Fine now; a cached column can come if it ever shows.
- A close guess can be faked by someone calling the API by hand. Barg buys only looks and isn't sold; look again
  before 9.5.
- A frame put on in the middle of a room shows after the next join.
- Migration 00015: 00014 is the parked players' maps' (branch `cld20261103_maps`), so that branch must renumber
  its migration above 00015 when it comes back.
