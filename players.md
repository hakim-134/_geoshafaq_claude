# Who plays Shafaq Geo

Context from the owner (2026-10-24), for every idea, text, picture and rule in the game.

## The people

- People of Uzbekistan, Tashkent first. Most of them are Muslims.
- Honest, simple, sincere people with good intentions. They want to grow, to become better
  people, closer to greatness, and they keep away from what is sinful, ugly or not
  recommended.
- They will play our game *"in name of God"*, as the owner put it. Opening it should never be
  something they regret.

## What it means for the game

**The game is not religious.** No religious words, symbols or themes in it: the owner was
clear that it should not mention religion. It has to suit these players, though: **decent and
legitimate.**

- **No gambling of any kind.** No bets, no paid random rewards (loot boxes, spins, mystery
  packs), no prizes of chance bought with money. The shop (`PLAN.md` Phase 9) sells fixed,
  visible cosmetics at a fixed price, and stays "never pay-to-win".
- **Clean.** Nothing vulgar or sexual, no alcohol, smoking or betting in the game's own
  pictures, avatars, emojis, names, sounds and texts (the guest emojis lost 🍑 and 🎲 on
  2026-10-24). Real street panoramas show the city as it is; that's fine.
- **Honest.** Real numbers only: no fake "players online", no invented reviews, no hidden
  costs, no tricks that push people to pay or to keep playing. The server scores, and nobody
  can buy a better score.
- **Respectful.** Nobody is mocked: not players, regions, languages or ways of life. Player
  names get the name filter and the report button (`PLAN.md` 3.7).
- **Good for the player.** The game teaches the city and rewards getting better. It doesn't
  try to eat people's time: no guilt for a missed day, no endless "one more".
- **Partners and ads**, if they ever come: never betting, alcohol or tobacco, nothing a family
  wouldn't want on their phone.

**A quick test:** would a decent family in Tashkent be fine seeing this on their child's
phone? If not, change it.
